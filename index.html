Hi, I'm Cora.

I'm an environmental geoscience and civil engineering double major at DePauw University, focused on why the ground does what it does and how to predict it.

I'm drawn to structural geology, hydrogeology, and environmental hazards. Next steps: complete my master's in civil engineering and build a career helping communities prepare for geologic hazards.
